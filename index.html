04

Same question, two cities

The expansion problem, concretely: ask “how many homes can go on a house lot?” in Victoria and in Vancouver and almost nothing lines up — different plan type, different zone names, different density math, different data plumbing.

Why this is the hard part of Urbora's roadmap A question engine that works in Victoria breaks in Vancouver in at least four places: (1) plan law — Victoria's OCP is a single bylaw under the Local Government Act; Vancouver has no LGA OCP and plans through the Vancouver Plan + area plans under the Vancouver Charter. (2) Vocabulary — “GRD-1 (PGA)” vs “R1-1” encode different ideas; a shared schema needs a translation layer, not a rename. (3) Density math — Victoria's SSMUH is unit-count based (Zoning Regulation Bylaw s. 52); Vancouver's multiplex is FSR-based (0.70, up to 1.0). (4) Data format — Victoria publishes ArcGIS MapServer layers with Zoning / ZoningBylaw / DPA_Number fields; Vancouver publishes through its own Open Data portal with different field names and update cadence. Adding a second city is a data-engineering project, not a prompt change.
