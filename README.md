# Ask Victoria 2050

Built by Sadhik Ahamed Shaik Mohammed — Cloud & Security Engineer at Absolute Security, Victoria, BC.

Pick a parcel in Fernwood or James Bay, Victoria, and ask in plain English what can be built there. Every answer cites the exact section of the adopted Victoria 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 25-045, adopted Oct 2, 2025), the zoning bylaw, or BC Bill 44 — and says when the answer is uncertain instead of guessing.

## What's in here

- 15 real parcels in Fernwood and James Bay, from the City of Victoria's public ArcGIS open data (address, folio, PID, lot size, zone, Development Permit Area, Heritage Conservation Area, and heritage status)
- Interactive map with the City's live zoning layer
- Plain-English Q&A with verbatim OCP quotes, section and page numbers
- A 20-question eval harness: tricky cases (Bill 44 vs OCP, heritage vs density, 2012 vs 2050 changes, out-of-scope questions) with pass/fail and citation accuracy
- Victoria vs Vancouver comparison — how the same question differs across the two cities

## Sources

- City of Victoria, Victoria 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 25-045, adopted Oct 2, 2025)
- City of Victoria Zoning Bylaw
- BC Bill 44 — small-scale multi-unit housing
- City of Victoria Open Data — parcels and planning layers (ArcGIS)

Data snapshot: Nov 19, 2026.

## Note

This is a demonstration, not legal or planning advice. Where the sources don't pin a number down, the demo says so instead of inventing one.
